Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview & Zero Data Collection

This Privacy Policy explains how FileVault ("the App", published by dotts apps, package ID com.aistudio.vault.wbexir) handles information. FileVault is built as a strictly local, offline-first personal organizer. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any of your personal information, documents, IDs, passports, notes, payment cards, or login credentials.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you enter or import remains exclusively inside your Android device's sandboxed app storage:

  • Documents, Scans & Photos: PDFs, scanned images, and files imported via Android's Document Picker or Photo Picker.
  • Personal Entries: Notes, ID card numbers, passport details, card entries, and login credentials you choose to save.
  • Profile Name & Preferences: Your display name and app lock preferences stored locally in Android SharedPreferences.

3. Biometric Authentication & Private Locker Security

  • Android BiometricPrompt: When Vault Lock is enabled, the App invokes Android's official system BiometricPrompt API (fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN). FileVault never accesses, reads, or stores your biometric templates or device passcode.
  • Private Locker Key: If you create a Private Locker, your 4-digit or 6-digit locker key is converted into a salted SHA-256 cryptographic hash on-device. The plain-text key is never saved.

4. Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA — Optional): Requested only when you tap to scan a physical document, receipt, ID card, or passport. Captured frames are cropped locally inside the app and never uploaded.
  • Zero Broad File/Gallery Permissions: FileVault uses Android's zero-permission Photo Picker and Document Picker, accessing only the individual file you explicitly select.
  • No Location, Contacts, Microphone, or Advertising ID permissions are requested by FileVault v1.0.

5. Third-Party Services, Ads & Analytics

FileVault v1.0 contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral analytics trackers, and no third-party data brokers. If optional cloud backup or advertising features are introduced in a future update, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated prior to release.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data lives solely on your device, you have full control at any time. You can permanently delete individual items from the Trash Bin, wipe all stored items from Me → Clear All Local Data, or uninstall FileVault from your Android device to remove all local app data completely.

7. Children's Privacy

FileVault does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
